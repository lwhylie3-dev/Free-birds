# Beastbound base models

Free 3D base models to retexture and reshape into the Beastbound cast. 82 files, GLB format.

All models come from Sketchfab under **CC-BY 4.0** or **CC0** (files fetched from the Objaverse mirror of Sketchfab). Models tagged "NoAI" by their authors were excluded. CC-BY means you may modify and publish them, including commercially, **as long as you credit the author** — keep the credits below wherever the models are shown.

These are stand-ins chosen from thumbnails. Fan art of existing franchises was filtered out by name, but check each model before publishing.

`PREVIEW.jpg` shows every model. "UV" means the model has a texture map to repaint; "rig" means it has a skeleton; "anim" is the number of animations.
