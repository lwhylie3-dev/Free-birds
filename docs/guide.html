Your private YouTube organizer

How Noxara YT works

A small web app of your own that reads your liked videos and playlists, learns what you're into, and sorts videos into private playlists. You approve every change first.

Short version: you don't install anything inside the YouTube app.

The playlists Noxara makes are ordinary YouTube playlists, so they show up in the YouTube app's You → Playlists tab like any other. To send it a video, you tap Share in YouTube and pick Send to Noxara.

Day to day

What happens, in order

  1. You, anytime

    Like or share a video

    Liking works on its own. Sharing to Noxara sorts it instantly and tells you which category it landed in.

  2. Every 30 min

    Noxara picks up new likes

    It reads the title, channel, tags and description, then matches the video to the closest category.

  3. When it's unsure

    It asks you

    Low-confidence videos land in "Needs review". Picking the right category teaches it for next time.

  4. Once a day

    It drafts a plan

    A list of exactly which videos would go into which playlists. Nothing changes yet.

  5. You approve

    Untick anything, then approve

    It re-checks your playlists so nothing is added twice, then adds the approved videos.

  6. In the YouTube app

    Open You → Playlists

    Your organized playlists are there, private, ready to play.

The YouTube app connection

Send videos straight from YouTube

Apple doesn't let web apps appear in the Share Sheet directly, so a tiny Shortcut does the hand-off. You build it once in the Shortcuts app (already on your iPhone).

First, get your token

In Noxara, open Settings → Create new token and copy it. Treat it like a password. If it ever leaks, tap the button again and the old one stops working.

Build the shortcut

  1. Open Shortcuts → tap + → rename it Send to Noxara.
  2. Tap the ⓘ (or the name at the top) → turn on Show in Share Sheet. Set it to receive URLs and Text.
  3. Add the action Get Contents of URL. Set the URL to your app's address plus /share:
    https://noxara-yt.YOUR-SUBDOMAIN.workers.dev/share

    Swap YOUR-SUBDOMAIN for yours. The exact address is shown on Noxara's Settings page.

  4. Tap Show More on that action. Method: POST.
  5. Headers → Add new header. Key:
    Authorization
    Value: the word Bearer, a space, then your token.
    Bearer YOUR_TOKEN
  6. Request Body: JSON → add a Text field. Key url, value Shortcut Input (pick it from the variables bar).
  7. Add Get Dictionary Value: key message, from Contents of URL.
  8. Add Show Notification with the Dictionary Value. Tap Done.

Limits

What it can and can't touch

Can

  • Read your liked videos and your playlists
  • Create new private playlists
  • Add videos to playlists, after you approve
  • Learn from your existing playlists and your corrections

Never / can't

  • Remove, move, unlike, or delete anything
  • Make anything public
  • See your password
  • Read Watch Later, Saved Shorts or watch history (YouTube blocks every outside app from these)
  • Change how the YouTube app itself looks or works

The daily YouTube allowance

Google gives each app 10,000 "units" a day for free. Reading is cheap, but each add costs 50, so a big first plan takes a few days. It continues by itself.

If something's off

Quick fixes

Google says "redirect_uri_mismatch"

The address in your Google sign-in client must match the redirect URI shown on Noxara's sign-in page exactly, including https and /auth/callback.

Sign-in stopped working after a week

The Google consent screen is still in Testing. Set it to In production, then sign in again.

The shortcut says "Invalid or missing token"

Check the header is exactly Authorization with value Bearer, a space, then the token. If you made a new token, update the shortcut.

It says the quota or AI allowance is used up

Nothing's broken. It pauses and picks up again the next day on its own.

I want out

Settings → Disconnect YouTube. That revokes access and deletes the stored login. Playlists it made stay yours as normal playlists.

Everything the app does is recorded on its Logs page.
